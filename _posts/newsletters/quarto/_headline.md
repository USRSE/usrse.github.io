## 🧳 San Jose or Bust: We're Going to USRSE26! 🧳

<a name="headline"></a>

The time has come! USRSE26 is finally upon us, and in just a few short weeks, the community
will descend upon downtown San Jose, California for several days of learning, connection, growth, and fun!
From poster sessions to keynotes, this year's conference is gearing up to be yet another 
exciting and edu-taining week for research software nerds of all backgrounds and flavors.

**Our keynote speaker this year is [Fernando Pérez](https://us-rse.org/usrse26/program/keynotes/#fernando-p-rez), Associate Professor of Statistics at the University of California, Berkeley, Faculty Scientist at LBNL, and — get this — co-founder of Project Jupyter!**

Among his many accolades, Pérez is a mainstay in the open-source software community, having contributed to
and led a dizzying array of projects, including IPython, Jupyter, and the Berkeley Institute for Data Science (BIDS).
Expect nothing less than an engaging and thought-provoking keynote from this research software luminary. Many
thanks go to Pérez and to the USRSE26 sponsors, listed [here](https://us-rse.org/usrse26/sponsors/), for making this year's event possible.

If this is your first time attending USRSE26, here are some attendance tips:

- Your go-to resource for all things USRSE26 is available at the [conference website](https://us-rse.org/usrse26/attend/).
- The conference will be held at the San Jose Marriott, located in downtown San Jose, California.
- We will kick off on **Monday, October 19, 2026** at 8:30 AM sharp with the opening keynote,
and officially conclude on Wednesday, October 21, 2026 at 5:00 PM with (my personally favorite part): Rapid Access Microtalks!
- In between, a variety of sessions will be held simultaneously, including workshops, talks, and paper, poster, and notebook sessions. Check the 
[conference program](https://us-rse.org/usrse26/schedule/) frequently to plan and pick what you'll attend.
- San Jose is a beautiful city with lots to see and do; the conference website has a handy 
[travel guide](https://us-rse.org/usrse26/travel/) to help you plan your trip, with a dedicated [Things to Do section](https://us-rse.org/usrse26/attend/local-info/).
- Be sure to review the [Photo Policy](https://us-rse.org/usrse26/attend/photos/) and 
[Code of Conduct](https://us-rse.org/usrse26/about/code-of-conduct/) before attending, and remember to be kind, respectful, and inclusive to all attendees.

**Attending but not presenting?**

Don't feel left out -- there are still ways you can get involved. Remember, the greatness of a community
is most accurately measured by the compassionate action of its members ([Coretta Scott King](https://www.goodreads.com/author/quotes/74494.Coretta_Scott_King)).
At the time of writing, the planning committee is still looking for volunteers to help with USRSE26, including
[chairing sessions](https://forms.gle/sTmHEDcFjGCqMkKy8). If you are interested in helping out, please reach out
to the conference organizers on Slack in the `#usrse26` channel, or contact the Planning Committee directly on
[the website](https://us-rse.org/usrse26/about/organization/).

In addition, you can do your part to hype up the event by sharing your conference 
experience on social media. Use the hashtag `#usrse26` to share your favorite moments, 
photos, and learnings from the conference with your colleagues and friends.

Lastly, if you are not attending, you can still support US-RSE's mission to advance the state of research software engineering by 
purchasing this year's [US-RSE merch](https://www.customink.com/g/gns0-00d3-mvw7) and taking it with you to rep the US-RSE community at your next conference destination!

The team is so excited to see you all in San Jose, California, in just a few short weeks!
