### New newsletter alert: User & Developer Experience

Our very own Hannah Coon and the UX working group will be sharing the latest and greatest developments in User and Developer Experience — UDX!

UDX in this case includes anything oriented toward making developers' or users' interactions with tech easier (be that via features, docs, policies, etc.). The ux-wg will also be highlighting relevant events, including talks/workshops/BoFs/posters at USRSE26. If you want your presentation included in the list, reach out to Hannah with the title and a very brief description of how it relates to UX or UDX work.
And, if you've got other relevant events or content you'd like promoted (work of yours or someone else's), send that along too!

## Community Spotlight

🌱 Our community is full of people doing fascinating research and software work, and we want to put a face to it. Starting this month, we'll be featuring a group of members in a regular spotlight: what they work on, a tool they can't live without, and how they found their way into RSE work.

We'd love to feature YOU. It takes about 5 minutes to fill out, and nothing gets posted without your okay: [complete the community spotlight form](https://forms.gle/dXqVsHKiHnot2u449)
