# Overview

The US-RSE Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) working group helps
strengthen inclusion, belonging, and equitable participation throughout the
US-RSE community. Diverse perspectives help strengthen research software,
research teams, and research outcomes. Our work is guided by the
[US-RSE DEI Mission Statement]({{ site.baseurl }}/about/dei-mission).

# Assessing Community Inclusion

The US-RSE DEI working group seeks to identify the barriers, opportunities,
and challenges that members encounter in their RSE roles, and to gather and
share insights that inform positive change within US-RSE.

As a new effort, we are piloting a **Community Inclusion Assessment** to better
understand member experiences and identify opportunities for improvement.

We want to hear from members. Please share your experiences and suggestions through the [DEI Suggestions Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdtCVAmJnUQCKnIXdn93xN5e9_zCQkrVdQsnPOiSBIcptma6w/viewform), in the [`#dei-discussion`](https://usrse.slack.com/messages/dei-discussion) channel on the US-RSE slack, or by messaging the [co-chairs](#co-chairs) directly on Slack.

# Advancing Learning and Awareness

The US-RSE DEI working group promotes diversity, equity, inclusion, and
belonging within US-RSE and the broader RSE community through education,
dialogue, and resource sharing.

## DEI Mission Statement

The US-RSE DEI working group's first large initiative was to create a US-RSE DEI Mission Statement to integrate into the US-RSE core values. This DEI Mission Statement was published in August 2021 on the [DEI Mission Statement page]({{ site.baseurl }}/about/dei-mission). We expect this mission statement to evolve and mature as the US-RSE community grows and welcome feedback.

## DEI Speaker Series

The US-RSE DEI working group occasionally invites DEI speakers to present on DEI topics.
Talks from all the speakers to date are available in the
[DEI Speaker Series playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLMgG8jy8w-8lUu8Uflt1WCsI8yzEEVybS)
on the US-RSE YouTube channel.

Suggestions regarding potential speakers are welcome,
by reaching out to the working group in Slack or through the
[DEI Suggestions Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdtCVAmJnUQCKnIXdn93xN5e9_zCQkrVdQsnPOiSBIcptma6w/viewform).

## DEI Media Club

The US-RSE DEI working group hosts occasional DEI Media Club discussions in which we invite participants to read, listen to, or watch the chosen DEI media and come ready to discuss the content as a small group. Past selections include the book *Better Allies* by Karen Catlin, series 4, episode 6 of *reWorked - The Diversity and Inclusion Podcast*, and [Code Switch - The Folk Devil Made Me Do It](https://www.npr.org/2021/08/20/1029775224/the-folk-devil-made-me-do-it).

These are announced via the [`#events`](https://usrse.slack.com/messages/events) and [`#dei-discussion`](https://usrse.slack.com/messages/dei-discussion) channels on the US-RSE slack, as well as in monthly newsletters, and are hosted virtually via Zoom.

## Resources for DEI in RSE

As part of its efforts, the US-RSE DEI working group is developing
a curated collection of
[RSE-specific DEI resources](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/e/2PACX-1vRwwTmM29KHDXd7s5clv1EcwITxPMNi7yIyaBUS_rwvJw87yHqgMDJU-kANFZQ1W2y3sz9GHoizmh7v/pubhtml?gid=0&single=true)
for improving diversity, equity, and inclusion specifically within the
research software engineering community. Please feel free to suggest
additional media and other resources through the
[DEI Suggestions Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdtCVAmJnUQCKnIXdn93xN5e9_zCQkrVdQsnPOiSBIcptma6w/viewform).

# Celebrating and Amplifying Diversity

The US-RSE DEI working group leads US-RSE's celebration of diversity throughout
the year through observances such as Black History Month, Women's History
Month, and Pride Month, including spotlight posts that recognize inspiring
individuals in computing, science, engineering, and math:

- [Pride Month]({{ site.baseurl }}/tags/#pride)
- [Black History Month]({{ site.baseurl }}/tags/#black-history)
