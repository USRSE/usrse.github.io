The seventh US-RSE Association Annual General Meeting (AGM) will take place on Friday, December 4th, 2026 at 1 PM ET / 10 AM PT.

#### Registration

The AGM is open to all US-RSE members. 
Members must [register in advance](https://zoom.us/meeting/register/XL7_0HzeQsGqzNLlC7Q4gQ).

Not a member yet? [Join](https://us-rse.org/join/) now!

This meeting will be recorded. 

#### Preliminary Agenda

1. **State of the US-RSE Association: The 2026 year in review and plans for 2027** 
  Ian Cosden, current chair of the Steering Committee, will give an organizational overview of the US-RSE including a review of past events and initiatives, updates on current initiatives, and an outline of some future plans and goals for the Association.
1. **Updates from Our Executive Director**
  Sandra Gesing will give an overview of the sustainability plan and organizational membership for US-RSE, recent activities, and plans for 2027.
1. **Updates from Working Groups**
  Representatives from US-RSE working groups will give brief updates on recent activities and initiatives for 2027.
1. **Meet the Steering Committee Candidates**
  The candidates running for the 2027-2028 Steering Committee seats will introduce themselves to the community via pre-recorded videos in preparation for the upcoming election. There will not be a candidate Q&A at the AGM; instead use the #election channel in Slack to ask the candidates questions.
1. **Financial Status and Budget Review**
  Julia Damerow, US-RSE Treasurer, and Abbey Roelofs, US-RSE Deputy Treasurer will provide an update of US-RSE finances and preliminary budget for 2027.
1. **US-RSE Q&A & Open Member Discussion**
  All are welcome to ask a question or raise a member-wide discussion topic. We will use both the chat and hand-raise feature in Zoom to facilitate the moderated conversation. 


If you have questions or suggestions of discussion topics, please contact [contact@us-rse.org](mailto:contact@us-rse.org) or reach out via [slack](https://usrse.slack.com/).
